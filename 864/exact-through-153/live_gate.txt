Let 𝐴 ⊆{1,…𝑁}
 be a set such that there exists at most one 𝑛
 with more than one solution to 𝑛 =𝑎 +𝑏
 (with 𝑎 ≤𝑏 ∈𝐴
). Estimate the maximal possible size of |𝐴|
 - in particular, is it true that
|𝐴|≤(1+𝑜⁡(1))⁢
2
√3
⁢𝑁1/2?

A problem of Erdős and Freud, who prove that
|𝐴|≥(1+𝑜⁡(1))⁢
2
√3
⁢𝑁1/2.
This is shown by taking a genuine Sidon set 𝐵 ⊂[1,𝑁/3]
 of size  ∼𝑁1/2/√3
 and taking the union with {𝑁 −𝑏 :𝑏 ∈𝐵}
.

For the analogous question with 𝑛 =𝑎 −𝑏
 they prove that |𝐴| ∼𝑁1/2
.

This is a weaker form of [840].
