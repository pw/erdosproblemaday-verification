Let 𝑓⁡(𝑛)
 be maximal such that in any 2
-colouring of the subsets of {1,…,𝑛}
 there is always a monochromatic family of at least 𝑓⁡(𝑛)
 sets which is closed under taking unions and intersections. Estimate 𝑓⁡(𝑛)
.

Let 𝐹⁡(𝑛)
 be defined similarly, except that we only require the family be closed under taking unions. Estimate 𝐹⁡(𝑛)
. In particular, is it true that 𝐹⁡(𝑛) ≥𝑛𝜔⁡(𝑛)
 for some 𝜔⁡(𝑛) →∞
 as 𝑛 →∞
, and 𝐹⁡(𝑛) <(1+𝑜⁡(1))𝑛
?

A problem of Erdős and Ulam.

It is trivial that 𝑓⁡(𝑛) ≥
𝑛+1
2
, since there is a sequence of 𝑛 +1
 many nested subsets. This seems to be all that they knew; Erdős [Er78] wrote 'we have no plausible conjecture for the true order of magnitude of 𝑓⁡(𝑛)
'.

They similarly had no good guess about 𝐹⁡(𝑛)
.

In [Er78] Erdős wrote that if the colouring is such that all subsets of same size receive the same colour then Howorka had proved that 𝐹⁡(𝑛) >𝑛𝜔⁡(𝑛)
 for some 𝜔⁡(𝑛) →∞
, but gave no reference.

With GPT-5.4 Pro I've managed to get some partial results that you can view here. There's not much literature around the problem.

We answer affirmatively the subexponential half of the problem.

The proofs are probabilistic, but the structural inputs are quite different. For 𝐹⁡(𝑛)
 the key observation is that the free rank of a union-closed family is exactly the VC-dimension of a complementary set system. For 𝑓⁡(𝑛)
 we use the fact that every family closed under union and intersection is a finite distributive lattice, hence isomorphic to an ideal lattice 𝐽⁡(𝑃)
 by Birkhoff's representation theorem. A large antichain in 𝑃
 yields a Boolean cube inside 𝐽⁡(𝑃)
, and after ruling out large cubes by random colouring one is left with counting bounded-width posets.
