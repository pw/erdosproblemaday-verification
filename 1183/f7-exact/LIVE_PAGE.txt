Let 𝑓⁡(𝑛)
 be maximal such that in any 2
-colouring of the subsets of {1,…,𝑛}
 there is always a monochromatic family of at least 𝑓⁡(𝑛)
 sets which is closed under taking unions and intersections. Estimate 𝑓⁡(𝑛)
.

Let 𝐹⁡(𝑛)
 be defined similarly, except that we only require the family be closed under taking unions. Estimate 𝐹⁡(𝑛)
. In particular, is it true that 𝐹⁡(𝑛) ≥𝑛𝜔⁡(𝑛)
 for some 𝜔⁡(𝑛) →∞
 as 𝑛 →∞
, and 𝐹⁡(𝑛) <(1+𝑜⁡(1))𝑛
?

A problem of Erdős and Ulam.

It is trivial that 𝑓⁡(𝑛) ≥
𝑛+1
2
, since there is a sequence of 𝑛 +1
 many nested subsets. This seems to be all that they knew; Erdős [Er78] wrote 'we have no plausible conjecture for the true order of magnitude of 𝑓⁡(𝑛)
'.

They similarly had no good guess about 𝐹⁡(𝑛)
.

In [Er78] Erdős wrote that if the colouring is such that all subsets of same size receive the same colour then Howorka had proved that 𝐹⁡(𝑛) >𝑛𝜔⁡(𝑛)
 for some 𝜔⁡(𝑛) →∞
, but gave no reference.
