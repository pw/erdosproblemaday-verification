What the seats found. Seven tool-equipped referee seats, each briefed to refute — five on disjoint stretches of the paper (§2–3 · §4 with the Lean's transfer · §5–6 · §7 · §8), one on the whole paper as a dependency graph with the Lean statement, one on sources and the world — between them covered all 62 pages from the rendered PDF, each scoped seat line by line through its stretch, and every one returned HOLDS AS READ: no FATAL, no GAP, a short list of cosmetic and exposition notes. Each seat rebuilt something and made it fail on purpose. The §2–3 seat verified the two unit-direction identities of Lemma 3.3 exactly in sympy (controls L → 2L, u → 2u fail), Lemma 3.4 exactly over ℚ(√2, i), Lemma 3.5 on 596 grid cases (the stronger bound w ≤ M fails in 109 of them), every constant in Lemma 2.7, and found the ergodic decomposition the brief suspected was missing written out on p. 22 — and it put its finger on the sentence that separates the algebraic plane from Payne's rational one: p. 13, in the proof of Lemma 2.7, "Each |xj| < 1 is a sum of two elements of K … The square roots belong to F", which is what forces ℤ[K] = E and fails for ℚ (1 − x²/4 must be a rational square; it is for 5 of 1,101 small rationals), with the same property used again on pp. 18, 19 and 21 and the divisibility of K only for the power maps of §3.2–3.3; it then built a 3-adic counterexample (3 is inert in ℤ[i], so rational rotations act isometrically on ℚ3(i); Haar measure on the closure of the rotation orbit of 1 there is invariant, ergodic, gives C mass zero and has Fourier coefficient 1 at z = 1) on which Lemma 2.7 fails at exactly that sentence and nowhere earlier, and noted Payne's two-colouring is 2-adic (colour by x + y mod 2; every rational unit step flips it, 1,260 vectors checked). The §4 seat checked Lemma 4.1 and Proposition 4.2 line by line, read the Lean's transfer end to end — measure k ω is the invariant law built from the actual colouring by iterated invariant means and Riesz–Markov; the interface is ProperColoring k c and nothing more; the Lean reaches positivity of the projection by a contraction argument instead of identifying a factor, formalises only the easy half of the spectral claim and never uses Bochner, gets a jointly measurable version correct at every point x rather than almost every x, and calls Theorem 2.3 at exactly one place, residual_fourier → wild_fourier, including the ergodic decomposition — and produced its own rational counterexample: run §4 itself on the proper two-colouring of the rational graph and the measure it manufactures is rotation-invariant, misses the continuous characters and is not Haar (its correlation is ½ or 0 by a parity that is discontinuous everywhere; exact fractions, 2,556 rational units, five controls). The §5–6 seat found where five enters (Proposition 5.6: a third label in a palette leaves the complementary pair, 5 − 3 = 2 — the bound is k − 3, so the argument is tight at five and breaks first there at six), checked the sixty-degree exclusion on the hexagonal 7-colouring (0 of 163,840 angles overlap; an improper colouring overlaps 2.9–4.2%), the expansions of Lemma 5.5 (remainder/d² at 0.70–0.72; a wrong-phase control grows like 1/d), the circle-measure Fourier decay (|J0(r)|(1+r)1/2 bounded, ≈ 1.15) and the five-cycle combinatorics (no solution once Lemma 6.7's bans apply, 40 without them). The §7 seat verified the planar cover obstruction by an exact SAT encoding of covers of discretised domains (UNSAT at multiplicity ≤ 2 and mesh ≤ 2 for disks of inradius 2.5 and 2.13; SAT, as it must be, at multiplicity 3, at mesh 5, and for a thin rectangle), corrected two things in our own brief (the obstruction is the paper's Lemma 7.5 on a square of side ten, and Lemma 4.3's first assertion is on Theorem 1.4's critical path — verified), and recorded that the Lean takes a different route through §7 (an acyclic crossing graph and fixed-radius centre disks instead of Lemmas 7.7–7.8 as written) so the kernel proves the theorem without certifying those paper steps. The §8 seat redid the Moser placement in exact arithmetic over ℚ(√3, √11) — all eleven edges of squared length exactly 1, the graph with no proper three-colouring, all seven vertices strictly inside the region of Lemma 8.8 with the tightest margin at vertex B, P′ − P ≈ 7·10−4 (certified ≥ 6.96·10−4), the paper's own §8.6 residuals reproduced to the digit, seven corruptions rejected — which agrees with our own exact check (the one finite piece we re-derived ourselves, with the region test failing at z = i/2 and z = 1 as controls), enumerated the 34 six-position words of Lemma 8.3, exhausted the five-cycle sign system (0 of 32,768 patterns) and noted that Proposition 8.2 does not actually need the palette bound it cites. {{WHOLE}} The sources seat checked 24 of the 33 references at the cited locators and found no mismatch (Falconer, Hadwiger and Woodall through secondary sources behind publisher walls, Zimmer's two by abstract, three by metadata only, Furstenberg 1977 closed access and not load-bearing), confirmed footnote 1 against Reed's May manuscript (his Lean takes the false π/3 density bound as a hypothesis; the half-open arc is a counterexample, and a unit-pair-free subset of the circle can in fact have measure π), and found the most material of the citations the paper is missing (the others being Székely 1984 and Shelah–Soifer on whether χ and χm can differ): Definition 1.2 is the independent-set notion for the circle-convolution operator of Bachoc–DeCorte–de Oliveira Filho–Vallentin (2014, Definition 2.1 and §3.4), as JoshuaZ had also pointed out.
