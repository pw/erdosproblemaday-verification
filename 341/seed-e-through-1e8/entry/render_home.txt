 Problem of the day &middot; Oct 14, 2026 
 Erdős Problem #377 
 OPEN number theory · binomial coefficients 
 The sum of the inverses of all primes smaller than \(n\), which don't divide the central binom coefficient. Is there some absolute constant \(C > 0\) such that \[ \sum_{p \leq n} 1_{p\nmid {2n \choose n}}\frac{1}{p} \leq C \] for all \(n\)? Statement as formalized in Formal Conjectures (Google DeepMind, Apache-2.0) &middot; their label: research open &middot; 4 further variants / known partial results in the Lean file. The authoritative wording is on erdosproblems.com/377 .
