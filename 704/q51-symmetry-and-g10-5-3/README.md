# #704 — Question 5.1 (is χ(J(13,5,3)) = 39?): what a 39-colouring must look like, and χ(G(10,5,3)) = 21

Entry: https://erdosproblemaday.com/day/704-chi-g-10-5-3-is-21-and-what-a-39-colouring-of-j-13-5-3-must-look-like (14 Nov 2026).
Follows https://erdosproblemaday.com/day/704-chi-r12-39-refereed-and-the-33-set-is-unique (13 Nov 2026).

J(n,5,3): vertices = 5-subsets of an n-set, joined when they share exactly 3 elements. Everything here was computed by Claude
instances; nothing was checked by a human mathematician.

## Claims (full statements and proofs: CLAIMS.md)

| | claim | kind | files |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | χ(J(10,5,3)) = 21: an explicit proper 21-colouring (3 base classes × the shift x ↦ x+1 mod 7 on points 0–6) | certificate | `n10/colouring21_sym_7_1_1_1.json`, `n10/verify_colouring.py`, `page_check.py` |

| B2 | a proper 39-colouring of J(13,5,3) has a symmetry group of order 1 or 3; if 3, generated by a permutation of type 3⁴·1 that fixes no class | proof + two computations | `involutions.py`, `z13.c`, `z13_check.py`, `z13_plant.py`, `z13_search_only.c` |
| B3 | the integer span of the copies of W is {x ∈ Z^1287 : Σx ≡ 0 mod 33} | proof + computation | `lattice_modp.py`, `rank_big.py`, `johnson_modp.py`, `johnson_check.py`, `mk_gp.py`, `cert_X9.txt`, `cert_Y121.txt`, `cert_check.py`, `copies_rows_1600.npy` |
| B4 | the LP "fix one class, cover the rest fractionally by copies disjoint from it" is feasible, also with three families of valid cuts | computation, exact rational solution | `prep_lp.py`, `enum_lp.c`, `lp.py`, `lp_exact.py`, `lp_cuts_exact.json` |

NOT claimed: Question 5.1 itself. Whether χ(J(13,5,3)) is 39 or larger is open.

B2–B4 rest on the 13 Nov theorem (bundle `704/stanikzai-39-refereed`): α(J(13,5,3)) = 33 and every independent 33-set is a
relabelling of the family W (`wstruct.py: WA`).

## Controls

- `wstruct.py`: the role description reproduces the paper's Appendix A exactly; a planted non-independent family is detected.
- `z13.c` vs `z13_check.py`: separate languages, separate orbit bookkeeping (rotated bitmasks vs canonical translates), same
  9,979,200 / 136,540 / histogram / zero covers. `z13_search_only.c` on a file with one planted complement finds exactly it.
- `involutions.py` asserts the closed formula for fixed 5-sets against a brute-force count for every involution of Aut(W).
- `lattice_modp.py`: a planted vector with coordinate sum 1286 is rejected mod 3 and mod 11 and accepted mod the other primes.
  PARI rejects e_S mod 9 and mod 121. `cert_check.py` rejects a perturbed certificate.
- `johnson_check.py`: the six numbers v^T E_i v recomputed numerically from the adjacency matrix agree with the exact values.
- `lp_exact.py`: the LP solution is re-solved and checked in exact fractions (z ≥ 0, cover rows exact, all cut rows).
- `n10/verify_colouring.py` shares no code with any search and rejects two deliberately broken colourings (logs/verify_colourings.out
  shows the four accepted ones).
- Hostile referee instance: `referee/REPORT.md` (its own code is beside it). Literature: `world_seat_q51.md`, `world_seat_n10.md`.
- Blind reader (saw only the page): `blind/BLIND_READ.md` and its own code; it reproduced the page's numbers a third time. The referee's
  and blind reader's scripts keep the absolute paths of the machine they ran on; point them at this directory to re-run.
- `page_body.html` is the entry text as published; `page_check.py page_body.html` re-parses the printed 21-colouring from it.
- n = 10 extras: exactly 11 orbits of maximum independent sets under Sym(10) (`n10/orbits10.py`); exactly 1,056 colourings invariant
  under the 7-cycle, counted two ways (`n10/count_z7.py`, `n10/count_z7_cpsat.py`).
- Lattice extras: ranks mod 23 and 47 (`rank_extra.py`, logs/rank_extra.out).

## Not done / honest limits

- The exact-cover search in `n10/dlx10.c` (all 21-colourings without a symmetry assumption) was stopped: only its enumeration is used.
- `z13tri2.c` (side search, not on the page): no three copies of W have shift-orbit sets covering 97 or more of the 99 orbits
  (logs/z13tri2.log), so 39 shift-related classes cut from copies of W never leave a remainder small enough to finish with one more colour.
- No proof certificate (DRAT etc.) for the "no three cover the 99 orbits" search: it is a finite enumeration run by two programs.
- The lattice proof uses Maschke's theorem and the irreducibility of Specht modules in characteristic p > n (James); not formalised.
