Transference for dilation graphs

We transfer a norm estimate for independent prime labels to an averaged moment of a dilation graph
whose labels must divide the integer at their vertex. Averaging a required divisibility condition
supplies a factor 1/p, which explains the reciprocal-prime law in the independent model. A prime
used at several vertices pays this cost only once. The main problem is to retain that dependence
while using the independent-model norm on most edges. The resulting moment will control pairings
in which one endpoint is constant on all mark lists at its integer position. Signed closed walks and
repeated prime labels also occur in the divisibility-graph arguments of Helfgott and Radziwiłł [11,
Section 1.5] and Pilatte [18, Sections 3 and 5]. The operators and ranges here differ; the memory
identity and transference estimate below are proved for the present model.
