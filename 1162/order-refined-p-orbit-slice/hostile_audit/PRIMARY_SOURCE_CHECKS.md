Both hashes equal the corresponding files in the author bundle. Direct source
inspection confirms:

- Theorem 1 gives the two-sided bounds with leading exponent `n^2/16`.
- Theorem 2 gives the p-subgroup leading exponent `p^(n^2/(4p^2))`.
- Theorem 6 gives the stated elementary-abelian-section and Sylow-2-order

- Proposition 7.3 constructs the lower bound from subdirect subgroups of a
  product generated by disjoint p-cycles, and fixes middle rank/order in that
  argument.
