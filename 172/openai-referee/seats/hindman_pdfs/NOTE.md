# Hindman 1980 vs 1984 — what is verified and what is not (2026-10-11, 06:36Z seat)

**1984, verified verbatim.** Hindman, *Partitions and pairwise sums and products*, JCTA 37 (1984) 46–60, DOI 10.1016/0097-3165(84)90018-9.
Abstract, from the Wayback snapshot of the ScienceDirect page (20240423065713, file alongside this note):
> "A seven cell partition of N is constructed with the property that no infinite set has all of its pairwise sums and products in any one cell. A related Ramsey Theory question is shown to have different answers for two and three cell partitions."
Hindman–Phulara 2013 (p. 1) and Hindman–Ivan–Leader 2022 cite THIS paper for the 7-colouring (see `../referee_citations.md`).

**1980, NOT verified.** Hindman, *Partitions and sums and products—Two counterexamples*, JCTA 29 (1980) 113–120, DOI 10.1016/0097-3165(80)90052-7.
