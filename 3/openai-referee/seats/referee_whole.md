### 1b. Order in which constants/parameters are chosen (as written), and the cycle question
Fixed k → s = k−2 →
 (i) D: §9.6 (pp. 75–76) / §10.2 (p. 77): absolute weights of a fresh patch are inflated only at the s active steps; at an active step the "other" weighted degree is bounded by "the bounded polynomial degree of the restored formula, the current lift weight j ≤ s, and determining weights at most s" (p. 76, ll. 4–6); Thm 8.2 says g♯'s degree is "bounded in terms of the input degree bounds only" (p. 62), and at passive steps "the restored absolute orbit is a polynomial of degree at most s in u alone" (p. 75). So the recursion (depth s; multiplier at step i chosen after the degree bound produced with the step-(i−1) multiplier) depends on s only. D := its output (≥ s). THEN "Fix all sampler, family-polynomial, and algebra degree cutoffs to cover these bounds" (p. 77). The sampler's degree cutoff (≥ D) is chosen after D and does not feed back into the weight recursion (the active-step inputs involve only weights ≤ s and the patch degree s; passive constraints enter only as mesh-controlled site fields, Def 8.1). → no D-cycle.
 (ii) n0(s), then ξ0 (gain of the fixed-dimensional absolute rule, D.3/D.5/C.1), then ε0 (D.7 target discount), then c, giving ξ with (1−ε0)(1+ξ0)(1−c) > 1+ξ (p. 12). D.7's exponent E depends on (s, n0, side ratio, ε0) only and "is independent of the absolute rule, of d, d0" (p. 123; I.15 p. 193). ξ does not depend on D.
 (iii) η_k: §10.4 "Choose the fixed proportional losses sufficiently small that a net factor 1+η_k remains" (p. 81). The losses are: preparation discount (total fixed, split over N_c cuts — this only drives γ), forward loss O(Dϵ) (Prop 6.2 proof, p. 44: ϵ chosen after D), flag losses (1−η)/(1+η) per active layer (9.1), per-layer extraction discounts (Prop 9.1), root compression (Lemma 10.1), coverage ratios (1+γ)^m. All are fixed fractions chosen after D and ξ and before any p-dependent quantity; γ is chosen later to make the coverage ratios meet them. Thm 8.2's losses are additive fractions of the score δ, not of the target, so they do not touch η_k.
 (iv) Given α: p = C(2 + log 1/α) with C large enough that every temporary threshold (a fixed multiple of a0 = α/2, the multiple coming from (iii)) is ≥ e^{−p} (p. 81). T = ⌈log(2/α)/log(1+η_k)⌉ + 1 = O_k(1+p).
 (v) d0 = (2+p)^{C_k} (Lemma 2.2 / Thm 2.1). Depends on p and k only. Lemma 2.2 enlarges D.7's resource parameter to poly(p+n) (n = terminal-box dimension) but "Its rank conclusion is d + d0, so the enlarged resource parameter does not enlarge the fresh rank d0" (p. 12) — consistent with D.7's statement, whose absolute-rule hypothesis carries the rank bound d0 separately from the budget p, and with I.15 ("replace the absolute rank bound by min(d0, p) ... The eventual rank estimate with the original d0 only becomes weaker", p. 193). No cycle: d0 is fixed before d*, and the terminal dimension n (≤ poly(d*)) affects only Lemma 2.2's costs, never d0.
 (vi) d*(p), N_c(p): Prop 10.2 (pp. 77–78), structural, from d0, T and the fixed polynomials P_i; "does not use a width, rank, scale, or accuracy parameter."
 (vii) γ = (fixed gain-dependent constant)/poly(2+p+d*+N_c) (p. 78). Uses (iii) and (vi) only.
 (viii) per round, descending j = D..1: U_j ≤ F_j(p, d*, N_c, log 1/γ, q_j, U_{>j}); N_c A_j + A'_j ≤ G_j(p, d*, N_c, log 1/γ, U_{>j}) (p. 78). Within level j: warm geometry (a_j, υ_j/w_j, minimum sides) → warm A_j, A'_j → only then cold-pre and late choices (rank threshold R_j, perturbation, range, sides) which may use q_j + N_c A_j (pp. 78–80; same order in §4.6 p. 35). Rank-stop probabilities at h charge only layers ≥ h (Prop 6.2 statement, p. 42).
 (ix) envelopes V_D, ..., V_1 (Prop 10.3, p. 80); root cutoff; log N ≥ their sum (Prop 10.4).
Inside the analytic appendices the orders are stated and, as far as I read, consistent: E.1.3 "Q, B0 and the first physical mesh; stability and cylinder budgets; b; final meshes and accuracies; then sufficiently large L and H_i" (p. 136); F.2 "(F.2) pre requirements; σ↓; A↑; R↑; min_i H_i↑" (p. 137) with the 14-step table (p. 177); I.15 "F_q(p) ≤ (2 + p + F_{q−1}((2+p)^{c_s}))^{C_s}", q ≤ s (p. 195).

### 1c. Is A_k iteration-independent by the paper's own argument? YES.
All per-round bounds are fixed polynomials (depending on k only) in (p, d*, N_c, log 1/γ, q_{>j}) (p. 78, Prop 10.3 p. 80); the forecasts d*, N_c, γ are fixed once for the T-round run; value spaces are reset to full ambient spaces each round (p. 77 "We may now reset every value space"), congruence moduli are converted into warm width losses at the root (Lemma 10.1), and centres are kept bounded, so no height accumulates across rounds. T enters each envelope linearly: V_j = Q_j(0) + T·P_j(p, d*, V_{>j}) with only D levels of composition (p. 80), so V_1 ≤ (2+p)^{C'} with C' depending on k (through D and the degrees of the P_j) only. Prop 10.4 then gives A_k = O_k(1). The paper also states it directly: "Polynomial exponents and the fixed gain are independent of the number of increments" (Thm 2.1, p. 10) and "with iteration-independent polynomial exponents" (p. 81). This holds GIVEN the warm/cold claims of the component statements (Lemma 4.2/4.3, Thm 3.4 warm clause, Prop 9.1, Thm 8.2), which are other seats' scope.

### 1d. Coordinator questions (trace worries)
(Q1) Trace Part I §26 (trace.txt ~l.1327): "a physical mesh depends on a truncated expansion whose bound already depends exponentially on the truncation parameter … leaves this circularity concern unresolved." In the final paper the matching structure is Appendix E.1.3 (Prop E.1): the residue expansion is truncated at level b (h_lo = Σ_{|S|≤b} h#_S, p. 132; "The number of terms, each term's cap, and the sum of caps are B(p) once b is fixed"), and a physical grid of I is used. The paper resolves the order explicitly: the FIRST physical grid is chosen from the Lipschitz bound of the fiber densities Φ_{t,t′} (cap e^{O(p)}B0, Lipschitz e^{O(p)}(1+B0)^2, p. 133), i.e. from Q, B0, ζ only — "Here Q, B0, then ζ and the first physical grid, are chosen before b" (p. 134, l. 2); the stability trees of Prop E.2 live on these cells; the b-dependent mode caps drive only a SECOND, finer mesh of the sample variables (x, V, t) and late side/CRT cutoffs: "The second, finer mesh only partitions the sample variables x, V, t; the site densities h# and their stability trees still use the original physical u cells, chosen before b. Thus neither the stability budget nor the no-increment slice cost depends on that finer mesh. Its smaller sample boxes require only later Chinese remainder accuracy and side-length cutoffs." (p. 136, ll. 8–13). The shell bound (E.10) B(p)2^{−(b−|I|)} has a prefactor fixed before b ("All those bounds, including |I|, were set before b. Choose b last", p. 135). The grid's absolute cell side J (needed for the O(nQ^{2b}/J) ≤ η marginal error) is large because H_i is chosen last; only the RELATIVE grid is chosen before b. So the circularity of the trace is resolved in the final text by an explicit two-mesh order; the parameter of the b-dependent mesh is chosen after b and is consumed only by late (side-length) choices. I checked the order, not every estimate.
 Other meshes/truncations in the main return (grep "mesh|truncat|net": pp. 51–59, 60–68, 161, 176–177, 184–188): §7.4 flag truncation states its order explicitly — truncation threshold T from "the unexpanded score and the current cap and accuracy, not those later menu sizes" and "Subsequent models and menus may charge log T" (p. 52, ll. 38–46); Lemma 7.5's field mesh "Its cost is warm at j, since only widths Qi with i > j enter" (p. 53, ll. 53–55); Def 8.1 meshes are "pre-logarithmic", and "No mesh is taken over the polynomial coefficients of gψ or over arbitrary site functions" (p. 54); I.2 function nets have "logarithmic size polynomial in p + log(1/η)" (p. 187). None of these is chosen from a quantity fixed after it, as written. (Whether "warm at j" is TRUE for Lemma 7.5's mesh is a §7 question for that seat.)
(Q2) Trace Part II §37 (~l.1985): "Need check thresholds from lower or same layer repeatedly charged as cost of width resetting w_i. Need independent Q_i." Final paper: Lemma 4.3 item 2 states "At weight h, the relative width loss uses only dimension and gap factors, independently of R_h, Q_h, and the size of the rational section used in the cut. At weights above h, no width loss is necessary. Lower relative losses are polynomial-log in their warm data and the cold data at h." (p. 26). The section vector e (height poly in dims, cut count, log R_h, §4.6 p. 35) is charged to LOWER levels ("The additional relative precision uses only dimension factors, γ^{−1}, w_h^{−1}, ∥e∥, and the coefficient bound in (4.1). For every lower level these are permitted higher-level costs", p. 27) and to the incoming copy, "not by conditioning the new j-residual" (p. 79). Prop 10.3's displayed recurrence is Q_j^out ≤ q_j + P_j(p, d*, q_{>j}) "where P_j is a fixed polynomial independent of q_j and of all lower width logs" (p. 80), proved from Q_j^out ≤ q_j + N_c A_j + A′_j and the G_j bound, which uses only U_{>j}.
