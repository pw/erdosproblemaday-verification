2. **[Br26, Section 2.5]** — A flags-entropy.tex: "This is the ordered version of Bradač's D* construction [Section 2.5]" → **MATCH** (D*(t,q) of §2.5: vertices (a,b) with ab ∈ E(G) i.e. a ⊥ b; arc (a,b)→(a',b') iff ab' ∈ E(G) and a'b ∉ E(G) — exactly OpenAI's rule i<j: a_i ⊥ b_j and a_j ⊥̸ b_i). B 00-introduction: "the ordered incident-flag graph of Bradač's higher-dimensional construction [Section 2.5]" and 01-foundations: "We use Bradač's ordered flag construction [Section 2.5]. … Sample an ordered stream of N independent uniform flags" → **MATCH with an EXPOSITION caveat**: §2.5 orders the *distinct* flags (all |V(D*)| ≈ q^{2t−1} of them) by a uniformly random permutation (Lemma 2.3) and counts forward-independent tuples deterministically; the i.i.d.-stream-with-repetition, N = ⌊q^dσ⌋ positions (a vanishing fraction of the ~q^{2d−1} flags), and the *selection-entropy* framing are OpenAI's. Bradač's v1 *sketch* (§2.2, for the weaker D) did use "N = c_s q^t (log q)^2 independently randomly chosen pairs", so i.i.d. streams are not foreign to him; but §2.5 itself is a permutation construction. Severity COSMETIC/EXPOSITION: B's "Bradač's ordered flag construction" attributes the stream variant to him; A's phrasing ("ordered version of") is the accurate one. Consistency definition (B base:consistency "i<j, a_i ⊥ b_j ⇒ a_j ⊥ b_i") is verbatim Bradač's "consistent" in the proof of Claim 2.13 (plus a_i ⊥ b_i). MATCH.
3. **[Br26, Claim 2.13]** (A compression-local.tex "adapts the marking argument of Bradač [Claim 2.13]"; B 04-marking.tex "adapts the counting argument in [Claim 2.13]") → **MATCH**. Source: "Claim 2.13. There is a constant A = A(t) depending only on t, such that for h = Aq^t and w = Aq log q, it is possible to mark some vertices of T such that every vertex of T has at most h marked children and every path in T starting from the root contains at most w unmarked vertices." The proof uses W^σ(y) = +{b_i | a_i ⊥ y}, r(y) = dim W(y), U_r, Z_r, popular/poor — B's "keep a vector subspace V(y) … adjoin its second endpoint b to V(y)" is visibly this W(y) bookkeeping (but adjoining only at *expensive* positions — an OpenAI modification, "freezing its state between expensive steps" as A says). The brief's paraphrase "all but O(q log q) positions marked in known domains of size O(q^d)" = h = Aq^t (t = d), w = Aq log q. MATCH.
4. **[Br26, proof of Lemma 2.12]** (B intro: "Bradač's direct count records O(qσ) expensive flags, with O(σ) information per flag [proof of Lemma 2.12]. The resulting O(qσ²) allowance is too coarse …") → **MATCH**. Source: Lemma 2.7 applied "with Δ = |V(D*)| ≤ 4q^{2t−1}, h and w", giving i_k ≤ 2^k (4q^{2t−1})^w h^{k−w} ≤ 2^k q^{2tw} h^k, "where … we used that k ≥ Cq(log q)^2 so that q^{2tw} ≤ 2^k". OpenAI's diagnosis of where the extra (log)^{s−2} comes from is accurate. (Bradač does not use the words "expensive flags"/"information"; that is OpenAI's entropy translation of the 2^k Δ^w h^{k−w} count — legitimate.)
5. **[Br26, Lemma 2.1]** (A flags-entropy.tex: "it also underlies Bradač's polarity-graph estimates [Lemma 2.1]") → **MATCH**: "Lemma 2.1 ([3]). ### Priority / novelty assessment — what is new in family 170 vs everything public before 2026-10-06 21:58Z
**Already public before 6 Oct (not new):**
1. The polynomial exponent s−1 for every s ≥ 3 and the log power 2s−4: Bradač v3 Thm 1.1 (16 June), formalized three times independently (Trellis ×2, Alexeev).
2. The construction: the incident-flag digraph D*(t,q) with t = s−1 and the edge rule a_i ⊥ b_j, a_j ⊥̸ b_i (Bradač v3 §2.5), its T_{t+1}/K_{t+1}-freeness (Lemma 2.11), the "consistent" sequence notion and the marking argument (Claim 2.13). **Per Bradač's own declaration, this s−2 → s−1 step was itself found by an internal OpenAI model** and given to him (communicated before 11 June). So the construction family 170 uses is, in origin, an earlier OpenAI-model contribution, published (with attribution) under Bradač's name — family 170's papers cite it as Bradač's and do NOT mention that OpenAI's model originated it (A and B intros attribute D*/§2.5 to Bradač only). EXPOSITION: a reader of the OpenAI papers alone would not learn that the §2.5 construction is an earlier output of the same programme; the attribution is nonetheless accurate (it is published in Bradač's paper, which says so).
3. The upper bound with power s−2 (AKS 1980; LRZ 2001); OpenAI's own elementary upper-bound proofs are re-derivations, not new results.
4. The *conditional* route to 2s−4 (Mubayi–Verstraëte) — and nothing anywhere giving a log power below 2s−4 for any s ≥ 5 (arXiv sweeps T3; erdosproblems; FC; teorth; Palomar; HN; MO; press). Bohman–Keevash/Spencer are polynomially weaker.
**New in family 170 (as claims):**
(a) the lower bound r(s,t) ≥ t^{s−1}/(log t)^{s−2+ε} for every fixed s ≥ 5 and ε > 0 (A: s = 5 with explicit t⁴/(log t)^{3+ε}; B: s ≥ 6), i.e. the log power improved from 2s−4 to s−2+o(1), matching AKS up to (log t)^{o(1)} — **no prior public claim of this found**; it remains a (log t)^{o(1)} short of the conjectured Θ(t^{s−1}/(log t)^{s−2}) (the ε is not removed; Chen notes removing it for s+1 would remove it for s).
(b) the method: replacing Bradač's container/marking COUNT (which pays O(qσ) expensive flags × O(σ) bits = O(qσ²), forcing k ≳ q log²q) by an entropy-compression argument for a *selected* consistent sequence in an i.i.d. stream (N = ⌊q^dσ⌋, k = ⌊qσ^{1+η}⌋), with sparse-pair descriptions, rich-line/plane polynomial bounds kept below the characteristic (EH16 regime), and, for s ≥ 6, repeated projection and a Nie–Wang rich-subspace bound. B's diagnosis of exactly where Bradač loses the extra (log)^{s−2} is accurate against Bradač's proof of Lemma 2.12 (T1b item 4).
(c) a Lean formalization of both (OpenAI's own; no third-party replay public).
**Redundancy observed by third parties:** B ⟹ A via the classical Shearer-1995 neighbourhood descent (Lin–Zhang Cor. 4.1; Chen §2), so the s = 5 paper is not logically needed once s ≥ 6 holds; and the r(4,t) = t³/(log t)^{2+o(1)} corollary (not stated by OpenAI) was derived by others within a week.
**Credit/plagiarism context:** Bloom's blog notes "accusations of plagiarism" and pre-release appearances for *some* proofs in the release; for family 170 I found **no** pre-6-Oct public text with the s−2+o(1) log exponent (Bradač's arXiv/Drive versions, the Erdős–Rogers paper of 29 Sep, the forum, FC, teorth all stop at 2s−4). The only priority entanglement is the reverse one: the construction OpenAI builds on was already (credited) OpenAI-model output inside Bradač v3.
