Seat W checked the record around the claim, with quotations and dates.

  • The belief. Zakharov's 2026 survey "Small triangles" says that "many believe (including Szemerédi) that it is 'almost' true: Δ(N) ⩽ CεN−2+ε" (§3) and calls it "the conjectured Δ(N) ≲ N−2+o(1)" (§6). Ben Green's open problem 77 asks the same thing as a question. The paper does not cite Green's list. Formal Conjectures states it as Green77.green_77, still open there. Our Extra507.lean proves its negation, from the every-n theorem through FC's own α. As a control, the same statement with exponent +2 in place of −2 is provable from α(n) ≤ 4, and we prove it. So FC's statement is false because α is large, not because of its form.
  • Earlier power claims. The paper describes Ellmann accurately and Agama in part.
    • Ellmann's arXiv 1703.03297 v12 calls its own argument heuristic.
    • Agama's arXiv 2006.05269 v13 builds its points from a regular polygon plus its centre. W found the paper's objection to it correct only in part, and too weak. The centre gives a collinear triple only when the polygon has an even number of vertices. The real failure is that three consecutive vertices of a regular m-gon span area of order m−3, which W checked numerically, so the claimed bound fails for every large polygon.
  • After the release. Two drafts claim much larger exponents by building on OpenAI's construction:
    • Ivan Blinov (littleBro/heilbronn-triangle-bounds, from 8 October), "conditional on the pinned manuscript's geometric estimates", up to η = 1/50,893,237;
    • Qiuyu Chen (19 October), η = 2/100,866,719, using Blinov's pieces.
    Neither has a complete Lean proof: Chen supplies none, and Blinov's Lean covers some of the finite and algebraic steps. Big O Speedrun's board lists both, and OpenAI's ≈ 3.18·10−236, as "Claimed". We have not checked either draft.
  • Big O Speedrun's statement. On 19 October Joshua Cortez proposed a trusted Lean statement for that board in pull request #20, BigOSpeedrun.HeilbronnTriangle.Bound η. It landed on their main branch as commit 079ac7b4 on 21 October. It says: for every large n, n points with every triangle ≥ c·n−2+η. Its own README notes that OpenAI's comparator theorem is weaker, a sequence of n only. Our Extra507.lean proves Bound at OpenAI's exponent 2/(45435k+16), statement copied verbatim and checked. Their record for the statement says it "proves no board record". Their gate does not yet take Heilbronn submissions, so nothing has been submitted.
  • Citations. Cohen–Pohoata–Zakharov's two theorems (arXiv 2305.18253 Thm 1.1; arXiv 2409.07658 Thm 1.8, published in Inventiones), KPS 1982's §2 and Roth 1951's appendix say what the paper says they say.
